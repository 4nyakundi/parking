Guard Quick Operation Guide

Mombasa Mall Basement Parking (60 Slots) • Mwongozo wa Walinzi

GATE STATION
1
Login with Your 4-Digit PIN
Ingia kwa Nambari Yako ya Siri (PIN)
When you start your shift, tap your 4-digit PIN on the screen keypad. Your name will appear at the top right corner. The tablet stays active for your full shift.
2
Driver Scans Billboard QR
Dereva Achanganua QR Kuingia
Driver scans the ramp billboard QR code with their phone. They enter their plate number and store name. The tablet will play a chime sound and display their card.
3
Tap "ACCEPT & PRINT"
Bonyeza "ACCEPT & PRINT" Kutoa Tikiti
Check that the plate number on screen matches the car. Tap the big green ACCEPT & PRINT button. Hand the thermal ticket to the driver. Parking is FREE.
4
Driver Has No Smartphone?
Dereva Hana Simu? Andikisha kwa Mkono
Tap the big green "+ ADD CAR MANUALLY" button. Type the plate (or tap Use Camera Plate), select their store (Naivas, Gym, etc.), and confirm. The ticket prints instantly.
5
Cars Leaving: Tap "CLEAR EXIT"
Magari Yanapotoka: Bonyeza "CLEAR EXIT"
Switch to CARS LEAVING tab. The exit camera will suggest the departing car. Verify the driver's ticket or search their plate, then tap the giant orange CLEAR EXIT button.
6
Printer Jam / Lost Ticket?
Karatasi Imekwama au Tikiti Imepotea?
• Paper Jam: Refill roll and tap REPRINT TICKET.
• Lost Ticket: Search plate in Cars Leaving tab.
• Unknown Car: Ask Supervisor for emergency override PIN.
